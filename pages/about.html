HORIZON OF CR7 is a personal tribute built around the journey of Cristiano Ronaldo. It brings together career memories, interactive football games, original comic-style visual collections and a small collector-card experience — preserving the feeling of an extraordinary football era in one place.

This collection is not an official Cristiano Ronaldo website. It is an independent fan-made project, created with admiration and respect.

1997 — 2026 · The journey so far

THE JOURNEY OF A LEGEND.

From a young boy leaving Madeira to a name known in every corner of the football world. Different clubs, different chapters — one relentless pursuit of greatness.

1997 — 2002

The first dream · Sporting CP

At just 12, he left home for Sporting's academy. The journey began with sacrifice, courage and a dream bigger than his surroundings.

2003 — 2009

A new stage · Manchester United

A gifted young winger arrived in England and grew into a global star, winning the Champions League and his first Ballon d'Or in 2008.

2009 — 2018

The Madrid years · Real Madrid

A remarkable chapter of goals, records and four Champions League titles. He became the club's all-time leading goalscorer.

2018 — 2021

A new challenge · Juventus

He carried his ambition to Italy, won domestic honours and continued to deliver on a different stage.

2021 — 2022

Back where it began · Manchester United

A return to Old Trafford brought another chapter with the club where he first became a world superstar.

2023 — PRESENT

A new horizon · Al-Nassr

A new football chapter in Saudi Arabia — still competing, still scoring, and still adding to a career that continues to evolve.

2003 — 2026 · PORTUGAL

For the shirt · For the nation

From his first senior appearance to captaining Portugal to the 2016 European Championship and two Nations League titles. In November 2026, he announced the end of his international career — a 23-year chapter with his country.

His Portugal international chapter has ended; this does not mean he has retired from all football. His club career with Al-Nassr is a separate, continuing chapter.
